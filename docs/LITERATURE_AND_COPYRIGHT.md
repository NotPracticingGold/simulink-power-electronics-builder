# Literature and copyright policy

## What must be uploaded

Only upload the skill files and repository documentation:

- `SKILL.md`;
- `agents/openai.yaml`;
- the three MATLAB helper scripts;
- `references/power-electronics-workflow.md`;
- README, publishing instructions, test script, `.gitignore`, and the license you select.

No academic PDF is required for the skill to function.

## What should not be uploaded by default

Do not upload these local source PDFs unless you hold redistribution rights or the exact copy has an open license:

- Xu Chengwu, *Research on Half-Bridge Three-Level LLC Resonant Converter and Its Control Strategy* (`半桥三电平LLC谐振变换器及其控制策略研究_徐成午.pdf`);
- Ilho Jeong and Byeongcheol Han, “Partial Power Processing Based Two-Stage Half-Bridge Resonant Converter With Reduced Component Count,” *IEEE Transactions on Power Electronics*, vol. 41, no. 6, 2026, DOI: `10.1109/TPEL.2026.3653486`;
- *HV Intermediate Bus Converter as Essential Link to Power Server Boards Directly From 800 V DC*;
- J. Kasper, *Power Conversion Solutions for Future Server Boards Operating Directly From High-Voltage DC* (2026).

Publisher access, institutional access, or purchasing a PDF normally does not grant redistribution rights. Linking to the DOI, publisher page, institutional repository, or legitimate open-access copy is safer than committing the PDF.

## What literature information may be uploaded

You may include bibliographic metadata and your own short, non-infringing summaries:

- title and authors;
- venue, year, volume, issue, and page range;
- DOI or stable public URL;
- a concise statement of which general modeling practice the source informed;
- your own parameter tables or diagrams only when they do not reproduce protected figures and when attribution is clear.

Avoid copied publisher figures, full-page screenshots, long verbatim excerpts, and extracted tables unless permission or an applicable license allows redistribution.

## Optional examples

If you later publish example `.slx` models, place them in a separate examples repository or release package. Include only models you created and are entitled to distribute. Document required MathWorks products and avoid bundling generated caches, `.slxc`, `slprj`, simulation result `.mat` files, or model backups.
